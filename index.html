Why It’s Built This Way

A few of the reasoning calls behind the structure.

One URL Per Product

Every product — including add-ons like the Manual and Motorized Drive Kits — gets its own page instead of living on one long homepage. Each page can rank on its own, and a customer can be sent a single link for exactly the product they asked about.

Coming Soon ≠ Dead End

The Gray Tank Bypass Valve and Trap Shield are patent-pending and not yet shippable — but their pages exist now with a notify-me signup, so EZRV starts building a launch list instead of the pages returning a 404 until release day.

External Buys Stay External — On Purpose

Checkout lives on shopMORryde.com. Rather than fight that with a redirect or a confusing embedded cart, every “Buy Now” is labeled clearly as an external link so customers aren’t surprised when the page changes.

Structure Mirrors How People Actually Shop

The three journeys — Discover, Purchase, Support — aren’t decorative. Every top-level section maps to one of them, so the nav matches the questions a visitor is actually asking, not an internal org chart.
